A series of **Power BI reports** created as part of a personal **LinkedIn project** titled "**Dashboard ReDesign**".



The goal was to help aspiring analysts develop a better eye for design and hone their UX/UI skills. I based the work on real-world reports, redesigning them according to effective dashboard principles—ensuring the report told a story rather than simply presenting a collection of colorful charts. Each analysis included a detailed explanation of the changes made and why the new approach was superior.



The project resonated strongly with the community; people saved and shared the designs, while analysts reached out asking me to review their own projects and point out errors.
